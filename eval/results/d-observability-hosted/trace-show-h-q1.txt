6.2 The faculty member may not hold the sole management role. A full-time chief executive or

6.3 No workload exemption is given to the applicant, and the start-up may not use students'
coursework in a way that creates a conflict of interest.
</passage>

<passage n="4" source="faculty-entrepreneurship-policy-2026-revision.pdf" section="1. Purpose and scope [1.1-1.2]" page="1">
1.1 The policy lets full-time faculty members take part in a technical start-up that
commercialises research carried out at the university, and sets out the conditions, the
application procedure and the reporting duties.
1.2 Faculty entrepreneurship means founding or managing a technical start-up, holding
significant equity in it, sitting on its governing board or receiving a share of its revenue.
</passage>

<passage n="5" source="faculty-entrepreneurship-policy-2026-revision.pdf" section="4. Procedure [4.1-4.2]" page="1">
4.1 Applications are submitted through the online portal of the research office (ORIC) at any
time of the year, with the prescribed form and a short business plan.
4.2 The Campus Director comments and forwards the application to ORIC, which evaluates it
within 30 working days and presents it to the Rector. The Rector's decision is final.
</passage>
