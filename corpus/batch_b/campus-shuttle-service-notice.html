Campus Shuttle Service Notice

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE: prepared for the RAHBAR RAGOps evaluation (Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES document; nothing in it applies to real students.

1. Routes and timings

1.1 The shuttle runs between the main gate, the hostel block and the city bus terminal on weekdays.

RouteFirst departureLast departure
Main gate to hostel block7:30 am9:30 pm
Main gate to city bus terminal7:45 am6:15 pm

2. Boarding rules

2.1 Students show their card to the conductor when boarding. Visitors may not use the shuttle.

2.2 Seats are not reserved. Students with a documented mobility need are given the front seats.

3. Changes during examinations

3.1 During the final examination weeks the last departure from the main gate moves to 8:00 pm. Any further change is announced on the notice boards one day in advance.
