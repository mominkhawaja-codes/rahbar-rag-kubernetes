Hostel Residence Policy (Fall 2026)

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE: prepared for the RAHBAR RAGOps evaluation (Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES document; nothing in it applies to real students.

1. Eligibility and allotment

1.1 Rooms are offered first to students whose permanent address is more than 50 km from the campus, then to other applicants in order of application date.

1.2 Allotment is for one semester and is renewed on request if the resident has no unresolved disciplinary case.

2. Fees and security deposit

2.1 The residence fee for the semester is set by room type and is paid with the tuition fee.

Room typeFee per semester
Single roomRs. 95,000
Double roomRs. 68,000
Triple roomRs. 52,000

2.2 A security deposit of Rs. 15,000 is paid once, when the first room is allotted. It is refunded within 30 days of the resident vacating, after deducting any damage found at the check-out inspection.

3. Hours and visitors

3.1 Residents must be inside the hostel by 11:00 pm from Sunday to Thursday and by 12:00 midnight on Friday and Saturday.

3.2 Visitors of the same gender are received in the lobby between 4:00 pm and 8:00 pm and may not enter residential floors.

3.3 A resident who will be away overnight informs the warden in writing before leaving.

4. Conduct

4.1 Cooking appliances, heaters and any item that needs a separate electrical connection are not allowed in rooms.

4.2 Residents keep common areas clean and report damage or faults to the warden the same day.

4.3 Repeated breaches of this policy lead to a warning, then a fine, then cancellation of the allotment.

5. Leaving the hostel

5.1 A resident who leaves before the end of the semester gives 14 days written notice. The residence fee for the semester is not refunded.
